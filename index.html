Privacy Policy for PDF Maker & Reader

Effective Date: October 13, 2026

1. Introduction

We operate the PDF Maker & Reader mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our app.

2. Permissions & Local Processing

Our app requests specific permissions to function properly:

  • Camera: Used only when you choose to capture new photos to convert them directly into PDF documents.
  • Storage / Media: Used to select existing images from your gallery to create PDFs, and to save generated PDF files to your device storage.
  • Files / Document Access: Used to locate and read existing PDF files when using the app as a PDF reader.

Important Note: All PDF creation, image processing, and file reading happen locally on your device. We do not upload your personal photos, camera captures, or documents to any external servers.

3. Third-Party Ads (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app to keep it free. Google AdMob may automatically collect and use certain data, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and coarse location data, to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.

You can learn more about how Google uses data by visiting Google's Privacy & Terms.

4. Data Security & Retention

Since your documents and images are processed and stored locally on your device, you retain full control over your files. You can delete your generated PDFs or clear app data at any time directly through your device settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can take immediate steps to remove it.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within this app and on our hosting page with an updated effective date.
